Questions

Which board and syllabus is this for?

RBSE – Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan (BSER Ajmer), Class 12 Physics (SS-40 paper, 56 marks theory). It follows the 14 chapters of the Rajasthan Board Class 12 Physics syllabus (NCERT-based).

Is it a copy of past papers?

No. Questions asked in earlier official papers (2020, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 CWSN paper) are summarised and tagged with the year; all explanations, solved examples and the 5 model papers are original. The book is not endorsed by or affiliated with BSER.

How reliable is the chapter-wise weightage?

Marks per chapter are an estimate from the board blueprint and past papers; sources differ by 1–2 marks for a few chapters. The book says so clearly. Always check the latest BSER syllabus/blueprint for your year.

Are the model papers in the latest pattern?

Yes – 56 marks, 18 questions: 18 MCQ (½ each), 10 fill-in-the-blanks (½), 10 very short (1), 10 short (1½), 3 long (3) and 2 essay (4), with internal choice (OR) in the long and essay questions. Each has a marking scheme.

Hindi or English – what do I get?

Two complete editions with the same content: Hindi medium (250 pages) and English medium (258 pages). Each is sold separately.

Does it cover practicals and sessional marks?

No. This book is for the 56-mark theory paper only.
