Questions

Which age group is this for?

Parents of children aged 2 to 16. Every day has separate tips for ages 2–5, 6–9, 10–13 and 14–16, plus special chapters on toddlers and teens.

Will it ask me to take the phone away completely?

No. The goal is healthy use, not zero screens. You will build screen-free zones, a family media plan and better habits – step by step, without daily fights.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is educational guidance based on child psychology and public-health recommendations (WHO, Indian Academy of Pediatrics, American Academy of Pediatrics). It includes red flags and Indian helplines for when to seek professional help.
